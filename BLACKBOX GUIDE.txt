-----------BLACKBOX GUIDE-----------


HOW TO LAUNCH GAME:
1. Go to Pico-8 Command Prompt
2. Type in "LOAD MAIN.P8"
3. Type in "RUN"


CONTROLS:
Move - UP, DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT Arrow Keys
Z - Switch Weapons
X - Shoot



FEATURES:
White Boxes - Touching them teleports you to another dimension
Random Events - Random scary events will happen at random times
